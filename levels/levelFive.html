ARCHIVE XIII RECORD: UNKNOWN

Before the Order had a name, there were those who WATCHED the eastern gate beneath the oldest tower. They were told to remain silent until the bells had finished their song.

On the first night, the keeper COUNTED three bells, though the tower contained four. No explanation was written, and none of the witnesses agreed upon what they had heard.

The second record instructed every member to SPEAK only after the candles had been extinguished. The instruction was considered strange, but the Order followed it.

At midnight, the seventh keeper was ordered to RETURN to the eastern chamber and retrieve the sealed book.

The book contained no prophecy. It contained only numbers, repeated names, and a single warning written beneath the final page.

Four witnesses claimed that thirteen candles had been burning. Three witnesses insisted there had only been twelve. The record preserved both accounts.

The keeper then wrote that every member should COUNT the doors before entering the chamber. There were eight doors, although only seven could be opened.

Those who entered were forbidden to READ the inscriptions above the doors. The inscriptions were said to change whenever they were understood.

The final witness claimed that the Order had hidden something beneath the thirteenth stone. He was told to FOLLOW the numbers written in the margins.

He never explained what he found. His final sentence simply stated that the beginning had been placed at the end, and the end had been placed at the beginning.

The archivist later instructed the next keeper to REVERSE the record before attempting to understand it.

Beneath that instruction was a symbol resembling XIII. No explanation accompanied it.

The final surviving page contained thirteen lines. Every line appeared ordinary except for one detail: the words were arranged with impossible precision.
